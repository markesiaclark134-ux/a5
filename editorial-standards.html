Journal practice

Editorial Standards

How we keep the publication useful, clear and responsible.

Purpose and scope

Our articles explain general watch concepts in plain language. We separate education from model-specific claims and encourage readers to confirm specifications with manufacturers or sellers.

Research and corrections

Writers compare established horological principles, current public documentation and practical ownership considerations. We avoid invented testing, unnamed endorsements and unsupported guarantees. If a material error is identified, we review the source and correct the article when appropriate.

Commercial clarity

The current website does not sell products and does not present editorial categories as investment opportunities. Future sponsorships, affiliate relationships or paid placements must be clearly identified near the relevant content.

Safety and specialist boundaries

Water resistance, authenticity, repair condition and value can depend on the exact watch and its history. Readers should use qualified professionals and current manufacturer instructions where those questions matter.
