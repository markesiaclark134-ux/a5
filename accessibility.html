Journal practice

Accessibility

How we keep the publication useful, clear and responsible.

Our approach

Timeless Watch Journal aims to provide clear navigation, readable contrast, meaningful image descriptions, keyboard-accessible controls and responsive layouts. Motion is reduced when a device preference requests it.

Known limitations

Typography and generated editorial images may display differently across devices. The contact form in this downloadable package is a demonstration until connected to an accessible form provider.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, contact us with the page, device and a short description. We will review practical improvements and prioritise issues that prevent access to core editorial content.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is treated as an ongoing responsibility. New pages and significant updates should be checked for heading order, focus visibility, link clarity, contrast and zoom behaviour before publication.
