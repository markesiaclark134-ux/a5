Privacy Policy

Plain-language information about how this editorial website operates.

Information we handle

Timeless Watch Journal is an editorial website. We may receive information you choose to provide through a connected contact form, such as your name, email address, subject and message. The demonstration form supplied with this downloadable website does not transmit data until the site owner connects a form service.

Analytics and consent

The website includes Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode. Analytics storage is denied by default until a suitable consent interface records a visitor choice. When enabled, analytics may process device, browser, interaction and approximate location information in line with Google’s documentation. The site owner should configure a compliant consent banner before public launch.

Purposes and lawful basis

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, correct editorial material, maintain security and understand aggregated site performance. Depending on the context, processing may rely on consent, legitimate interests or steps requested before providing a service. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and sharing

Enquiry records should be retained only as long as needed for response, legal obligations and security. Necessary providers may process information for hosting, analytics or communications under appropriate terms. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify a request.

Security, children and changes

Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards should be maintained, though no online system is completely secure. This website is not directed to children. Material updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
