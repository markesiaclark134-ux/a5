Shipping Policy

Plain-language information about how this editorial website operates.

No physical fulfilment at present

Timeless Watch Journal is currently an editorial information website. It does not accept orders for watches or other physical goods and therefore does not presently offer shipping, delivery estimates, tracking or collection.

Editorial references

A discussion of a watch type, material or ownership practice is educational and does not mean that the item is stocked or sold by us. If you follow a link to another provider, review that provider’s identity, shipping destinations, charges, duties, delivery estimates, insurance, signature requirements and returns before purchasing.

Future fulfilment terms

If physical goods are introduced, each offer will disclose dispatch timing, delivery regions, available methods, charges and important restrictions before checkout. International customers will be told when taxes, customs duties or import handling may be separate from the displayed price.

Delays, loss and damage

Future order terms will explain how to report delay, loss or transit damage, what evidence may be required and which party is responsible at each stage, subject to applicable law. This page will be revised before shipping activity begins.

Contact

For questions about the present editorial website, use our contact page. For goods purchased from another seller, contact that seller directly.
