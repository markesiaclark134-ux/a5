Refund Policy

Plain-language information about how this editorial website operates.

Current website model

Timeless Watch Journal currently publishes free editorial information and does not sell watches, subscriptions, digital downloads or paid services through this website. Because no payment is collected, there is ordinarily no purchase to refund.

If paid products are introduced

Before any paid offer is made available, the relevant product page and checkout must clearly state the seller, price, currency, delivery method, cancellation rights, refund eligibility, exclusions, time limits and contact process. Those transaction-specific terms will control alongside applicable consumer law.

Third-party purchases

Editorial references do not make us the seller of a third party’s product. Refund, return, warranty and cancellation requests for purchases made elsewhere must be directed to the named seller under that seller’s policy.

Incorrect charges

If you believe a future service operated by this website has charged you incorrectly, contact us with the date, amount and non-sensitive transaction reference. Do not send full payment-card information. We will investigate and respond within a reasonable period.

Statutory rights

Nothing on this page limits consumer rights that apply under relevant law. This policy will be updated before commerce is enabled.
